pixel-hunter-night

Proposed color principles and a faded blue-cyan palette for the pixel-hunter Herdr layout. Text and signal colors come from Lospec palettes (Vinik24, Chasm, Cryptic Ocean; mood reference Eulbink). Those palettes have no near-black blue-cyan, so the dark ramp is Vinik24's #387080 darkened.

The principles are adapted from Valve's published art direction: the illustrative-rendering approach in Team Fortress 2 (Mitchell, Francke & Eng, NPAR 2007) and the readability rules in the Dota 2 character art guide. Valve wrote them for 3D characters in busy scenes. Here they are applied to a sidebar full of agents.

Record of design question Q23, 2026-11-05. Status: proposal.

Principles

01 · READ ORDER

Silhouette, then value, then hue, then detail

Valve designs characters so you can tell them apart by shape alone, then by light and dark. Hue comes third, and texture is last.

Each hunt state has a shape as well as a color (⚔ ! ✓ z, a frame around Needs help). Turn on Grayscale below: the states still read.

02 · QUIET WORLD

The environment is muted so characters pop

TF2 keeps backgrounds low in saturation and contrast, with soft detail, and saves the strong colors for the players.

Chrome, surfaces and the arena floor are low-chroma purple-navy and dark green. Strong color appears only on Hunters, the Monster and status signals.

03 · SATURATION BUDGET

The brightest thing is the most important thing

The Dota guide puts the highest value and saturation at the focal point and keeps the rest quieter.

Needs help #f3a787 (peach) is the lightest, warmest signal, because a hunter waiting on you is the one thing you must not miss. Its row also gets a frame and a blinking !. Cleared and Hunting are a step below it. Resting gets no color at all.

04 · WARM vs COOL

Warm advances, cool recedes

TF2 shades in warm and cool, with warm light on characters and cool shadow in the environment, to separate figure from ground.

Only two colors are warm, and both want you: Needs help (peach) and Monster HP (dusty rose). Everything else is cool: Hunting (faded blue), Cleared (faded green), focus and rank (mint), and the whole environment ramp.

05 · HUE-SHIFTED RAMPS

Shadows are never neutral gray

Valve's lighting tints shadows instead of darkening toward black. Pixel art does the same with hue-shifted ramps.

The 7-step ramp runs from deep blue-cyan slate to sage-cream text. No #000, no #fff, no #888. Text is about 9:1 on the chrome, not 15:1, so it reads clearly without glare.

06 · TEAM COLORS

Few signal hues, never reused

TF2 has two team colors, and they are never used for anything else.

Each signal hue has one meaning. Rose is only Monster HP, never an error or a branch. Mint is only focus and rank. Status hues never decorate.
