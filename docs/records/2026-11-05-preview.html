pixel-hunter preview

A mock of the full Herdr window with real Ninja Adventure sprites (CC0, pixel-boy & AAA), plus galleries of Hunters, Monsters and Areas. Click a font below and the whole page switches to it. Design record, 2026-11-05.

Party state: Font: Pixelify Sans

Font comparison

All fonts are OFL on Google Fonts, so they can ship with the layout. Each font gets its own size, because pixel fonts only look sharp at their native grid. Click one to apply it to the whole page.

Herdr window

320 px sidebar. Area shop-api is open with four Hunters in its Party. Click a Hunter card to see how focus moves.

Revised later on 2026-11-05: Area rows have three lines (space name, path, branch) beside a 76 px Monster face. The map name and HP bar appear only in the arena, so the row no longer repeats them.

Hunters (Class)

Each Hunter is a 16×16 sprite shown at 3×, facing right toward the Monster, with a 38 px face. The pack has only one frame per direction for Attack, Jump and Item, so motion is made by swapping frames and moving the sprite by a few pixels.

Monsters

Bosses are 40 to 68 px with real multi-frame Idle and Hit animations. Hit plays while the Party is Hunting.

Revised later on 2026-11-05: this gallery now shows all 12 bosses in the pack, including the six the app doesn't use yet, so one can be picked from the list. The Red Warlord was drawn wrong: its frames are 96×48 (swords out to the sides), not 48×48 squares, so each frame showed half the samurai. The 96×48 frames fix it.

Small monsters

Added later on 2026-11-05: all 58 small monsters in the pack. Each is a 16×16 sprite (the same size as a Hunter) shown at 3×, walking down. These have no Idle or Hit strips, only a 4-frame walk per direction.

Areas

Each arena is 160×96 art px built from the pack's tiles and shown at 2×. Following Valve's "quiet world" rule, every arena is desaturated and sunk toward the canvas color so the sprites stand out.

Found while building this

  • The floor tileset has snow and an orange (lava-like) ground, plus snowy trees. The Snow and Volcano Areas we postponed may need much less work than expected.
  • Hunter animation is thin. Idle, Attack, Jump and Item are one frame per direction. Only Walk has 4 frames. Hunting is shown as an Idle and Attack swap with a 6 px lunge.
  • Monster sizes vary (Frog 40 px, Tengu 68 px). The arena aligns them by their feet.
  • Giant Spirit is a fireball, which reads more like a Volcano monster than a Dungeon one. Once Volcano exists it can move there, and Dungeon can take Demon Cyclops or a skeleton.
  • The Hunter character carries a bow, so it works as the Archer.
