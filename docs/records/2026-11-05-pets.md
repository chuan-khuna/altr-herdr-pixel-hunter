# 2026-11-05: Pets (subagent tracking) and sidebar clean-up

Discussion in a grilling session, held in Thai, one question at a time. Visual results: `2026-11-05-hunter-row.html` (layout F) and the revised Area rows in `2026-11-05-preview.html`. Spec: `.scratch/pixel-hunter-pets/spec.md`. Implementation doc: `docs/documents/pet-tracking-hooks.md`.

## Discussed

- How to show the subagents an agent starts, framed as "a subagent is a pet of the agent, max 2 shown, then `[+xx]`".
- Duplicated information between the arena and the Area rows.
- Which details a Hunter row should show.
- Monster variety after a hunt ends.

## Decided

**Pets**
1. A Pet is a Claude Code subagent (Agent/Task tool) inside a Hunter's agent. It is not a Herdr `hc run` sub-agent.
2. A Pet is a Party member with no Class or Rank. A Hunting Pet keeps HP wearing and the Monster alive (no Slain).
3. A Pet's animal is picked at random once, from 10 small Ninja Adventure animals. A Hunter's live Pets never repeat an animal until all 10 are in use.
4. States: Hunting and Needs help only. A Pet disappears when its task ends, so it is never Cleared or Resting.
5. A Pet that Needs help is as loud as a Hunter that Needs help: peach frame, Area auto-expand, peach Area dot.
6. Crash safety: a Pet with no report for 2 minutes (configurable), or whose Hunter's terminal is gone, is dropped.
7. Nested subagents are flattened into Pets of the same Hunter.
8. In the sidebar, Pets show as a line of animal icons under the Hunter, max 2, then `[+N]` (layout F). This replaced the earlier choice of indented Pet rows. Hover shows type and task. Click focuses the Hunter. The arena shows 2× sprites at the Hunter's feet, max 2, then `[+N]`.
9. Pets do not appear in pane headers or tabs.
10. Pets come from a one-time Claude Code setup: hooks plus `subagentStatusLine`. No setup, no Pets.

**Hunter row (layout F)**
11. A state-colour strip on the left. The tab name is the Hunter's name, with the Class dim after it. The program is shown only when it isn't Claude Code.
12. Context shows as used/total tokens (`128k/200k`), as Claude Code shows it. The bar stays Stamina (what's left).

**Area row and Monsters**
13. Area rows have three lines (space name, path, branch) beside the 76 px Monster face. The map name and HP bar are removed, and the arena keeps them.
14. An Area's first Monster is its map's own. Each Respawn picks a random Monster other than the one just Slain, kept across restarts.

**Docs**
15. Records live in `docs/records/`, documents in `docs/documents/` (with `INDEX.md`). HTML docs share `docs/styles/pixel-hunter.css`. The rules are in `AGENTS.md`.
